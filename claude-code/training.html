What We Cover

The eight things every Claude Code session covers

Workshops are customized to your team and your work. These are the foundation topics that show up in almost every session, in some form.

Topic 01

What Claude Code is, and is not

Plain English. Where it fits compared to ChatGPT and Claude.ai. When to use it, when not to. The mental model that makes everything else click.

Topic 02

The "talk to your files" pattern

The most useful pattern for most SMBs. Point Claude Code at a folder. Get summaries, extractions, organization, and rewrites of everything in it.

Topic 03

Writing prompts that produce work, not just text

The prompts that get Claude Code to actually do something measurable. The structure, the context to include, the things people miss.

Topic 04

Multi-step jobs that run unattended

How to set Claude Code on a multi-hour task and walk away. The simple guardrails that keep it on track.

Topic 05

Hooking it into your existing stack

Your CRM, your Drive, your email, your accounting software. Live walkthrough of the integrations that matter for your business.

Topic 06

Building small custom tools

How a non-developer can have Claude Code build them a quoting calculator, a dashboard, or a lead scorer in an afternoon.

Topic 07

Cowork vs Code: when to use which

The desktop app vs the command-line tool. Which member of your team uses which. We set both up live in the session.

Topic 08

Safety, privacy, and what to never paste in

The handful of rules every team needs around client data, contracts, and confidential information. Two minutes of training that saves a lot of pain later.
