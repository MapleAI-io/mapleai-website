In Plain English

What is Claude Code, actually?

The technical answer: Claude Code is Anthropic's command-line AI agent. It reads your files, writes new ones, runs commands, and can do work across your entire business directory, not just one chat at a time.

The plain answer: It is a smart assistant that lives on your computer and can actually do the work, not just talk about it. Tell it to organize your client folder, pull data out of 200 PDFs, build a quoting calculator, write follow-up emails based on yesterday's CRM exports, and it does it.

The newer cousin, Claude Cowork, is the same engine wrapped in a friendly desktop app for non-technical operators. No terminal, no commands. We set up whichever fits your team.

"Most SMB owners we talk to know AI could help them. They have struggled to turn a chat window into something useful for payroll, invoices, marketing, or month-end close. Claude Code is the bridge. We are the people who actually wire it up."

Anthropic launched Claude for Small Business in May 2026 and is running a US-only workshop tour through fall. There is no equivalent program in Canada. That is the gap we fill.

Why It Matters For SMBs

What Claude Code does that no other AI tool does

A regular ChatGPT or Claude chat is a conversation. Useful, but you still copy and paste, click through tabs, and stitch the work together yourself. Claude Code skips that.

Reads your real files

Point it at a folder of contracts, quotes, invoices, or call notes and it works with the actual content, not just what you paste in.

Runs commands on your machine

Connects to your CRM, your spreadsheets, your Outlook, your Drive. Does the multi-step work, not just suggests it.

Works on long tasks

A regular chat forgets after a few messages. Claude Code can run for hours on a single goal and remember everything along the way.

Builds you tools

It can write a quote calculator, a lead scorer, a custom dashboard. Tools sized to your business, not enterprise software.

Connects to your stack

Hooks into ActiveCampaign, GoHighLevel, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Salesforce, Shopify, and more through MCP and Zapier.

Keeps your data on your machine

Files do not get uploaded to a third-party server. Important for law firms, healthcare admin, and anyone handling client data.

Use Cases

What we actually build with Claude Code for SMBs

These are not theoretical. They are the kinds of jobs Claude Code handles well, sized for a small business.

Law Firms

Contract review and clause extraction

Drop 50 contracts into a folder. Claude Code pulls every indemnity, every renewal date, every payment term into a structured spreadsheet. The kind of work that took a paralegal a full day.

Real Estate

Listing research and comparable reports

Point it at MLS exports and recent sales data. It assembles comparable reports, flags pricing gaps, and drafts client-ready summaries in your voice.

Trades & Contractors

Quote drafting from site notes

Voice memo from a site visit plus your standard pricing sheet equals a draft quote, customer-ready, before you are back in the truck.

Sales Teams

CRM follow-up at scale

Export from your CRM, Claude Code segments stale leads, drafts personalized follow-ups in your voice, and logs the activity back. Five hours of work, ten minutes.

Operations

Month-end and reporting

Pull from QuickBooks, your bank exports, and your project tracker. Get a clean month-end summary and a list of invoices needing chase. Every month, automatically.

Marketing

Content production and repurposing

One long-form piece, broken into a week of LinkedIn posts, a newsletter, a podcast outline, and three short videos. All in your voice, drafted overnight.
