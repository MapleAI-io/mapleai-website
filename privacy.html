Privacy Policy

Last updated August 14, 2026

The short version

Maple AI is a one-person consultancy in Orangeville, Ontario. We collect the minimum we need to reply to you and to understand how the site is used. We do not sell your information, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not send marketing email to people who did not ask for it.

Who we are

Maple AI (also operating as Maple AI & Automation) is owned and operated by Shawn Lowry in Orangeville, Ontario, Canada. For anything in this policy, contact sales@mapleai.io or call 905-999-3465.

What we collect

When you fill in the contact form. Your name, email address, phone number if you provide one, and whatever you write in the message. That is it. There are no hidden fields.

When you browse the site. Standard analytics through Google Analytics 4: pages viewed, approximate location at the city level, device and browser type, and how you arrived. This is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

We do not collect payment details through this website. Client invoicing runs separately through Stripe, which handles card data directly and never passes it to us.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if it goes further, to quote and deliver work
  • To understand which pages are useful so the site can be improved
  • To meet record-keeping obligations for work we are engaged to do

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Who else touches your data

A short, honest list of the services involved when you use this site:

  • Netlify hosts the site and processes contact form submissions
  • Google Analytics 4 provides aggregate usage statistics
  • Microsoft and Google email services carry the message once it reaches our inbox

Each of these holds data under its own privacy terms. We do not pass your information to anyone else, and we never sell it.

Cookies

The only cookies set are the analytics cookies described above. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings and the site will still work normally.

How long we keep it

Contact form enquiries are kept for as long as the conversation is live and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back. If you become a client, project records are kept for seven years to meet Canadian tax and business record requirements. Analytics data follows Google's standard retention period.

Your rights

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your information, where we are not required to keep it
  • Withdraw consent for any future contact

Email sales@mapleai.io and we will action it within 30 days. There is no charge.

Client data we work with

This is worth stating plainly, because it is the question clients actually ask. When Maple AI builds systems, we often work inside a client's own tools and data. That work is governed by the engagement agreement, not by this policy. Our standing practice is read-only access first, written approval before any system writes back, and no client data used to train anything.

Security

The site runs over HTTPS. Credentials for client systems are held in encrypted storage and are never committed to code or shared over email. No system is perfect, and we will tell you promptly if something goes wrong that affects you.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the updated version is posted here.

Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your information, contact us first at sales@mapleai.io. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
